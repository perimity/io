Who we are

Perimity LLC ("Perimity", "we") builds software for the cyber insurance industry. We are based in the United States, and our products are intended for business use by insurance professionals in the United States.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not show advertising.

This website

The pages at perimity.io are informational. They have no sign-up forms, no analytics scripts, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

Two outside services see your IP address and browser details when you load a page, as any web host does: GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

Subjectivity Tracker

Subjectivity Tracker (tracker.perimity.io) helps insurance brokers keep track of the conditions a carrier wants satisfied before binding. Here is what it stores.

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • If you sign up with a password, a protected (hashed) version of it. We cannot read your password.
  • If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the basic profile details that provider shares with us, such as your name and email address. We do not receive your Google or Microsoft password, and we do not get access to your mailbox, files, or calendar.

The records you create

  • Submissions: the client name and the carrier name you enter.
  • Subjectivities: the description of each item, its due date, and its status.

Emails you forward

You can forward a carrier email to your Tracker intake address so that Tracker drafts the subjectivity items for you. When you do:

  • Our email provider receives the message and holds it so Tracker can read it.
  • The subject line and body are sent to an AI model, which drafts a list of items. See How AI is used.
  • Tracker saves the subject line, the address the email was sent from, and the drafted items. Tracker's own database does not keep the body of the email.
  • Nothing is added to your records until you review the drafted items and confirm them.
  • Email from an address that does not match a Tracker account is ignored.

Reminder emails

Tracker emails you a summary when items are overdue or due within three days. Those emails include the client name, the carrier name, and the item description, so they pass through our email provider.

Cookies

Tracker uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Information about your clients

Carrier emails and submission records often name your clients and describe their insurance placements. You decide what to enter or forward, and you are responsible for having the right to share it with us. We handle that information only to provide Tracker to you. We do not use it for our own marketing, and we do not contact your clients.

How AI is used

Tracker uses an AI model from Anthropic for one job: reading an email you forward and drafting the subjectivity items it contains. The email's subject line and body are sent to Anthropic for that purpose.

  • The AI only drafts. You review and confirm every item before it becomes part of your records.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • Records you type in by hand are not sent to the AI model.

How long we keep data

  • We keep your account and your Tracker records for as long as your account is open.
  • Drafted items from forwarded emails are kept after you confirm or dismiss them, until you ask us to delete them or your account is deleted.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account details and all of your Tracker records within 30 days.
  • Our service providers may keep their own copies, such as received emails and system logs, for a limited time under their own retention schedules.

Export and deletion

  • Export: each submission in Tracker has a Print / Export view you can save or print. If you want a complete copy of your records, email us and we will send one.
  • Correction: you can update the status of your records in Tracker. For anything else you cannot change yourself, email us.
  • Deletion: to delete specific records or your whole account, email us from the address on your account. We will confirm when it is done.
  • Sign-in connections: you can remove Tracker's connection to your Google or Microsoft account at any time in that provider's account settings.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Each broker's records are restricted to that broker's account by rules enforced in the database itself.
  • Incoming email notifications are verified as coming from our email provider before they are processed.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Tracker is a tracking aid. It is not a system of record, so keep the original carrier correspondence in your own files.
