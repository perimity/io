What it checks

Six questions behind one “Yes.”

The application asks whether MFA is enforced. MFA Analysis answers the questions an insurer would ask after a claim.

Is everyone enrolled?

Registration

Which users have MFA set up, and which methods they use, from phishing-resistant keys to text messages.

Is it actually required?

Enforcement

Whether a policy requires MFA for each user, or whether it only exists on paper.

Who is left out?

Named exclusions

Every account excluded from MFA, by name, and the policy that excludes it.

Are admins protected?

Admin access

How many Global Admins there are, whether each is covered, and with what method.

Did MFA really happen?

Real sign-ins

Who signed in with a password alone in the last 30 days, not just what the settings say.

Is there a way around it?

Legacy sign-in

Whether old protocols that skip MFA are blocked, and whether anyone still uses them.

Works best with Microsoft 365 Business Premium. Registration and sign-in evidence need Entra ID P1, which Business Premium includes. Without it, Pulse reports what it can verify and marks the rest “not assessable,” never as a fail.

How it works

Minutes to connect. Nothing changes in your tenant.

  1. 01

    Connect

    A Microsoft 365 admin signs in and approves read-only access. Pulse never changes a setting.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Pulse reads MFA configuration and the last 30 days of sign-in activity, and scores each finding against CIS Controls 5 and 6.

  3. 03

    Share

    Get the report for your role, and share a copy with your broker or insurer through a private link.
