How it works

Minutes to connect. Nothing changes in your tenant.

  1. 01

    Connect

    A Microsoft 365 admin signs in and approves read-only access. Pulse never changes a setting.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Pulse reads configuration and recent sign-in activity, and scores each finding against CIS Controls.

  3. 03

    Share

    Get a report for your role, and share a copy with your broker or insurer through a private link.
